# Projects

A project holds everything for one brand or campaign: one Swatch, your Yarn, and the Patterns you have built from them.

## What a project is

A project is a plain folder on your computer. You can open it, copy it and back it up like any other folder. Nothing is hidden in a cloud account.

## Several brands

You can keep as many projects as you like. Each has its own Swatch, so a post for one brand can never pick up the colours of another.

## Inside a project

Each section of a project, Patterns, Brief, Yarn, Swatch, Parts and Knits, has a tab, and each tab says what it is for when you point at it. Each can be sorted into folders of its own (see [Folders](/docs/folders/)), shown as a grid or a list, sorted, and searched.

The trail at the top says where you are, such as Projects, then the project, then Yarn, then a folder. Each step goes back there, and you can drop files on a folder's step to move them into it.

## Start from a website

When you make a project, give the business's website, and knitted sets the project up from it: a swatch from the site, a profile of the business for your model, and pictures of every page. See [The Brief](/docs/brief/).

## On the Projects screen

Each project is a card. Open its menu, or right-click it, to rename, duplicate, archive or delete it, or to choose its picture: one of its images, or a new one you upload. In Yarn you can also open an image and choose **Use as project picture**. Your model can set it too, with `set_project_picture`.

# Patterns and the editor

A Pattern is a reusable layout. You build it once in the editor, then knit it as often as you like.

## Making one

In a project's **Patterns** tab, press **New pattern**, give it a name and pick a size. A project made with the **Social starter pack** comes with five Patterns ready: Story, Post, Square, Landscape and Banner. Each Pattern's menu has **Open in editor**, **Ask your model...**, **Rename**, **Duplicate**, **Move to folder** and **Delete**.

## The editor

The canvas is in the middle. Down the left is a row of tools, and each opens a panel:

- **Patterns**: the other Patterns in the project, and the **Layers** of this one. In Layers you can select, hide, lock and reorder Stitches.
- **Yarn**: your files. Click one to add it, or drag it onto the canvas.
- **Text**: a headline, subhead, body or eyebrow.
- **Shapes**: a rectangle, circle, line or stitch row.
- **Parts**: reusable pieces. See [Parts](/docs/parts/).
- **Voice**: a voiceover and music. See [Voiceover](/docs/voiceover/).
- **Swatch**: the brand's colours, to apply to what is selected.
- **Ask**: a conversation with your model about this Pattern. See [Using your model](/docs/using-your-model/).

On the right, the **Inspector** shows what is selected: its position and size, its slot, its text or picture settings, its animation and its style. Either side folds away for more room, and `Ctrl+\` (`Cmd+\` on a Mac) folds or opens both.

## Working on the canvas

- Click a Stitch to select it. Shift+click adds to the selection.
- Drag to move it. Drag a handle to resize it, or the round handle above it to turn it.
- Double-click text to edit it in place. Click away or press Ctrl+Enter to keep it, and Escape to cancel. Wrap one word in stars, like `*sale*`, for the brand's serif accent.
- Arrow keys nudge. Ctrl+D duplicates and Delete removes.
- Scroll to move about the canvas, or drag the space around the frame. Hold Space, or turn on the **Hand** (press H), to drag from anywhere, even over a Stitch. Dragging with the middle button works too.
- Pinch, or hold Ctrl and scroll, to zoom about the pointer. Ctrl+= and Ctrl+- zoom in and out, and so do + and - on their own. The zoom menu in the top bar has **Fit to screen** (Shift+1 or Ctrl+0), **Zoom to selection** (Shift+2), and 50%, 100% (Shift+0) and 200%. Double-click the space around the frame to fit it again.

**Keyboard shortcuts**, in the top bar's menu, lists the rest. On a Mac, use Cmd where it says Ctrl.

Every change saves by itself. **Undo** and **Redo** are in the top bar. **History** lists every saved change, yours and your model's, with **Restore** to go back to any of them.

## Locking

Lock a Stitch if it carries the brand: the logo, the colours, the type. Select it and turn on **Locked to the pattern** in the Inspector, or press the lock beside it in Layers. Fill mode, a batch and your model cannot change a locked Stitch. You still can, in Pattern mode.

On the canvas, a locked Stitch has a dashed orange outline. A free one shows a solid outline when you point at it or select it.

## Slots

Each Stitch can have a slot name, such as `headline` or `photo`, set under **Slot** in the Inspector. Slots are how Fill mode, a batch and your model fill a Pattern without touching the layout. Use lowercase, and each name once in a Pattern. Names stay put when the layout changes.

## Pattern and Fill

The **Mode** switch in the top bar has two sides. **Pattern** lays out the design. **Fill** only offers the free slots, to change the words and pictures. See [Fill mode](/docs/fill-mode/).

## Sizes

A Pattern is not tied to one size. Positions are stored as fractions of the frame, so the same Pattern can be knitted as a Story, a square or a wide ad. Change it with the **Size** menu in the top bar: Story, Post, Square, Landscape, Banner, or **Custom** for any size from 16 to 8000 pixels a side. The Knit dialog can make several sizes at once.

## Knit

Press **Knit** in the top bar to make files from the Pattern. See [Batch knitting](/docs/batch-knitting/).

# FAQ

## Is knitted free?

Yes. It is open source under the MIT licence.

## Does it work offline?

Yes. Fonts, tools and the renderer all live on your computer. The only thing that goes online is a model you choose to connect.

## Is the desktop app released?

Yes, for macOS, Windows and Linux, on the [download page](/download/). It is early, so expect rough edges.

## Which models work?

Any model or app that speaks MCP, such as Claude Code. The studio's own model uses a key you bring.

## Can my model break my brand?

No. Locked Stitches cannot be changed through the tools. A refused change is reported back.

## Where are my files?

In a folder on your computer. By default it is `.knitted-studio` in your home directory.

## Can I use real album art or other brands' logos?

See [Brand and legal guidance](/docs/brand-and-legal/). The short version: only with permission.

## Why the knitting words?

A Pattern is something you set once. A Stitch is one piece of it. Knit is the verb. It keeps the ideas straight. See [the vocabulary](/docs/the-vocabulary/).

# Voiceover

Voiceover turns the words in a Pattern into spoken audio and lays it under your video.

## Local by design

The speech comes from a model running on your own computer. Your script is not sent anywhere. knitted talks to it through a standard speech endpoint, so a base address is all it needs.

## Mixing

The voice sits on top of any music, and the music dips when the voice speaks. The final loudness is matched so your reel plays at a sensible volume.
